# Sample Risk Analyser and CAR Steps: Annual Review

## Purpose

This anonymised operational example explains how to complete a Kanaan Investment Risk Analyser and Client Advice Record (CAR) in KCAS. It covers the adviser, independent-review, issue and signature steps without including client data.

The KCAS structured record is authoritative. A separately prepared analyser must not be issued where its figures, bands, formatting or proposed advice do not agree with the verified KCAS case.

## Open the case

Menu path:

`Clients -> Select client -> Advice history -> Select advice case`

Expected initial status: `Draft`.

## Step 1: Verify the controlled case position

Confirm that the draft records:

- the correct advice type, adviser and advice date;
- the selected, versioned advice methodology;
- the calculated and final risk score and profile;
- dated sources for every material financial fact;
- all investments considered in the advice;
- current income, portfolio value, tax, liquidity, cost and material-risk explanations;
- the products considered and the exact recommendation; and
- whether a new investment, replacement, transfer or income change is proposed.

Where the corrected previous-form methodology is used, its half-open bands are `11-<20`, `20-<40`, `40-<55`, `55-<75`, and `75-85`. The expected-income answer concerns salary, pension, rental, business or other external income. A proposed increase in withdrawals from invested capital is not an increase in external income.

## Step 2: Adviser reviews the recommendation

The responsible adviser must review the complete draft, especially **Products considered and recommended** and **Client Advice Record**.

The recommendation must state the actual decision, identify affected investments, and explain suitability against the client's needs, objectives, liquidity, tax position, costs, risk capacity and selected profile. Unsupported market forecasts must not be used as the advice rationale.

## Step 3: Resolve independent-review findings

Open each finding under **Independent review findings**. Correct the structured record first, then record a resolution describing what was verified or changed.

Do not select **Accept with reason** merely to bypass a suitability or document-quality issue. Use **Resolved** only when the advice content supports the conclusion. Replaced external documents must be identified as superseded and must not be sent as the final controlled record.

## Step 4: Save and submit

1. Select **Save draft**.
2. Confirm the methodology, score, profile and recommendation.
3. Confirm that no review finding remains open.
4. Select **Submit for independent review**.

The status changes from `Draft` to `Ready for review`.

## Step 5: Perform the independent review

The preparer must not approve their own case. An appropriately authorised independent reviewer must:

1. verify the material fact sources;
2. recalculate material amounts and percentages;
3. confirm the Risk Analyser answers and result under the selected methodology;
4. compare the recommendation with the client's facts, objectives, liquidity and existing investments;
5. verify the tax, cost, liquidity and risk wording;
6. ensure every finding has a meaningful resolution; and
7. record a decision reason and select **Approve for issue**.

Approval freezes the structured facts and calculations and generates the controlled PDF.

## Step 6: Inspect the controlled PDF

Select **Download approved PDF** and confirm that it contains:

- the advice subjects and adviser;
- dated material fact sources and investments considered;
- client needs, financial position and limitations;
- every Risk Analyser answer, score and selected methodology;
- products considered and recommended;
- recommendation and suitability rationale;
- costs, tax, liquidity and material risks;
- independent-review details; and
- client and adviser signature lines.

If the document is incorrect, do not issue it. Return the case or create the necessary revision.

## Step 7: Issue and record the signed document

After the approved PDF has actually been sent, select **Mark as issued**. Do not mark it issued merely because the PDF was generated.

When the signed record is returned:

1. save it in the controlled client folder;
2. verify the client and adviser signatures and dates;
3. enter its full path under **Signed PDF path**; and
4. select **Record signed PDF**.

KCAS records the signed file metadata and hash and changes the case status to `Complete`.

## Completion test

An advice case is complete only when:

- the adviser has approved the recommendation recorded in KCAS;
- no independent-review finding remains open;
- a different authorised person has approved the case for issue;
- the KCAS-generated controlled PDF was sent to the client;
- the issue action was recorded;
- the signed client and adviser record was returned and linked; and
- the status is `Complete`.

The reusable sequence is:

`Draft facts and recommendation -> adviser decision -> resolve findings -> independent review -> approve -> generate controlled PDF -> issue -> record signed PDF -> complete`

Codex may gather evidence, reconcile calculations, draft the recommendation and record quality findings. It does not replace the financial adviser's advice decision, independent review, or client acceptance.
