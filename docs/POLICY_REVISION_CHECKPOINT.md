## Review Method and User Requirements

For one document at a time:

1. Identify the exact predecessor, its date/version, and the current 2026 draft.
2. Check that the draft expressly identifies the predecessor on which it is based.
3. Compare concepts, operational facts and useful details, not merely wording.
4. Present a discussion table: predecessor fact/concept; current treatment;
   omission, reduction, legitimate correction or verified relocation; significance.
5. When a detail moved elsewhere, identify the actual named document and section.
   Read that destination to verify it contains the detail. A reference to an
   unspecified schedule is not evidence that the schedule exists or contains it.
6. Discuss the findings before editing. Only make changes the user authorises.
7. For each legislative obligation, verify the actual applicable primary source
   and paragraph. Use genuine Word footnotes with superscript markers, normally
   numbered automatically, and normal footnote text. Preserve the existing
   regulatory/source-document list unless a change is expressly necessary.
8. Distinguish mandatory requirements, existing confirmed Kanaan practices and
   optional policy choices. Do not invent practices or promise more than law
   requires. Proportionality does not cancel express legislative requirements.
9. Preserve practical predecessor information: contacts, locations, controls,
   responsibilities and recovery instructions. Do not force staff to use the
   superseded plan to find essential information.
10. Research existing documents before asking questions. State what they say and
    ask specific confirmations, not broad questions already answered elsewhere.
11. Do not label everything historical/unverified merely because a draft awaits
    approval. Identify genuine contradictions or missing facts narrowly.
12. Preserve originals, update the 2026 draft, render and inspect the final layout,
    then report changes, remaining issues and page count. Remain on that document
    until the user agrees to proceed. Update this checkpoint incrementally.
