## Preserve and Verify When Revising

| Control delegated from the wider risk plan | Verified current destination | Future revision check |
|---|---|---|
| Entity AML/CFT/CPF risk assessment, appetite and treatment | BRA sections 3-6 | Retain business-specific exposures, inherent/residual distinctions, risk treatment, approval and review. Keep BRA's 1-3 likelihood/impact multiplication separate from the wider FAIS plan's 1-10 average and the client methodology. |
| CDD, identity, legal persons, trusts and ownership/control | RMCP sections 6-8; BRA section 4, R1 | Preserve proportionate client/party requirements and the actual evidence route. Historical or low-value trusts are not automatically high risk; do not remove express statutory duties through proportionality. |
| Delivery-channel and sensitive instruction authentication | BRA section 2 and section 4, R2 | Preserve direct engagement and independent authentication using contact details held independently of the suspect instruction. Do not reintroduce blanket callback/reply requirements for every routine email or assume replying to an email authenticates it. Check corresponding RMCP engagement provisions. |
| Source of funds and source of wealth | RMCP section 9; BRA section 4, R3 | Always establish and record the activity generating the funds and how they are invested. Supporting documentation is proportionate to risk; unusual/inconsistent facts require enquiries. Preserve risk-based wealth checks and applicable enhanced requirements. Do not collapse the two concepts or require the same wealth audit for every client. |
| PEP/PIP enhanced measures and sanctions/TFS checks | RMCP sections 10-11; BRA section 4, R5 | Preserve enhanced measures and relevant KI approval, screening records and follow-up. Evidence should distinguish actual reviewer, date, scope and result from mere document availability. |
| Ongoing monitoring and examination of unusual activity | RMCP section 12; BRA section 4, R6 | Preserve monitoring of flows, withdrawals, switches and changed bank/beneficiary details, and written examination of complex/unusual activity. Client compliance is not a forensic audit of every historical fund. Record plausible supported history and genuine unresolved issues honestly. |
| Incomplete/doubtful CDD and escalation/reporting | RMCP sections 13-14; BRA section 5 | Preserve pause/decline/termination and reporting consideration, prescribed periods and confidentiality. Do not invent reportable events or certify reports not actually submitted. |
| goAML checks, primary/backup responsibility and evidence | RMCP section 15; BRA section 4, R5 | Preserve business-day checking, Andries as primary, Andre as backup and Gert's oversight, unless a controlled role change is approved. Retain who/when/outcome including nil results; review actual operation, not just a policy promise. |
| Records, statutory retention triggers and legal holds | RMCP section 16; Annexure C | Preserve applicable minimum retention from the correct statutory trigger, retrievability, holds and accountability. Do not turn five years into a blanket period from file creation. |
| Third-party administrator responsibility and record access | RMCP section 17; BRA sections 2 and 4, R7 | Retain Kanaan's responsibility, actual agreements/access and money-flow model. Third-party administrators/platforms do not mean Kanaan recommends third-party products. IronTree supplies backup only, per later user clarification; do not repeat the July factual confirmation's broader description. |
| Training, employee screening and assurance | RMCP sections 18-20; BRA section 4, R7 | Preserve role-relevant training and competence evidence, employee integrity/competence and proportionate monitoring. Do not assert a dedicated independent AML audit or universal quarterly report duty where the operative arrangements do not support it. |
| New products/technology, change control and annual review | RMCP sections 21 and 23; BRA section 6 | Preserve material-change assessment, annual/earlier review, exact approved versions and approval/evidence. Actual client-assessment results should inform the business risk review. |
| Practical acceptance gates, red flags and evidence registers | RMCP Annexures A-C | Keep the operating gates and evidence types usable. A reference to KCAS alone is insufficient: locate the actual records and retain auditable exceptions and unresolved items. |

## Revision Procedure

- [ ] Compare the then-current BRA and RMCP to this table and the risk plan's section 9/Appendix C.
- [ ] Check the applicable primary legislative sources and genuine footnotes.
- [ ] Verify practical evidence and any changed business facts or assigned roles.
- [ ] Record any removed or relocated control, its reason and exact replacement destination.
- [ ] Update related risk-plan cross-references and controlled document versions together.
- [ ] Obtain the required approval; drafting alone is not approval or proof of operation.

HR schedules, IT operating cadences, advertising checks and cyber oversight metrics
retained in the wider risk plan are not wholesale transfers to the BRA/RMCP.
Keep the statutory AML programme focused and avoid unnecessary duplicate policies.
